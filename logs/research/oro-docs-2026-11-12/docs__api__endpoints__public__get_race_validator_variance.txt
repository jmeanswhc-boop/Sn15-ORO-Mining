Per-validator variance for a race
Per-validator variance statistics for a race, computed over included SUCCESS evaluation runs (`is_included = true`, `invalidated_at IS NULL`) joined via `eval_work_items` on `phase = 'RACE'`. Only agent-race pairs where at least two validators produced a run are counted.

Validator deltas and spread statistics remain available live. Mean scores are null while race scores are embargoed, including during a running race and its post-completion reveal window.

Announced 2026-07-17 as part of the validator-variance visibility commitment. See the miner announcement thread for background.

A race's committed world seed and its drand beacon (ORO-2756)
Published from race creation: `sha256(secret)` and the drand quicknet round the world seed waits for (the first at or after the qualifying close plus the offset). The beacon's signature appears once recorded; the secret, the world seed, the pinned world's pack hash and every rejected compile attempt appear when the race's results are revealed. 404 for a race without a commitment.
