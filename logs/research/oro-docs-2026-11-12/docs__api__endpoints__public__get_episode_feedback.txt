Get miner-safe episode feedback
Return a positive-allowlist `oro.episode_feedback.v1` projection of a single envpack episode: outcome, paid reward, step + tool counts, termination code, agent-visible interaction, and observable-only issue codes, plus `failure_category`: why the episode failed or lost reward. Grading checks, the raw ledger, reward record and explanation are never exposed here.
