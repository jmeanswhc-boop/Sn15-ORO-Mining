The next important weakness is the transition from a blocked decision to a completed repair. V3 can recognize a stockout or preserve a negative review, but it has no dependable, affordable way to resolve the resulting state once the full conversation becomes too expensive. Its guards can therefore prevent a bad purchase while leaving the session unfinished. Other branches force an unchecked replacement or decline with an empty cart. Raising the monetary limit or weakening the guards would not resolve these opposing behaviors.

Five tasks did not finish:

1. t-13e82f2c08, wrap skirt: contradictory reviews become a permanent checkout block.

The first review rejects a 230-cent cart because a 98-cent skirt, and another cheaper listing, allegedly satisfy the wrap requirement. The planner swaps to the 98-cent listing. The next review rejects that listing, treating its skirt_style value as a different property from its general-style field. The planner disagrees and repeatedly proposes checkout of the same cart. These proposals are locally held; they are not repeated real store checkout failures. The planner nevertheless describes them as store refusals.

At the end, the negative review remains cached, five environment steps remain, $0.009087545 remains in the agent allocation, and no new public action is executed. Eleven model calls occur after the final environment action, costing $0.009003195; this includes the final diagnostic review, so not every one of these calls is intrinsically unnecessary. The observed fault is lack of resolution/progress once the decision is disputed. These traces do not establish which conflicting property interpretation is correct.

The relevant functions are G5A.cart_check, BudgetControls.cart_review_context, BudgetControls.terminal_recovery_hold, E9.turn_loop and E9.run. Caching a boolean plus prose does not record the proposition that was rejected or a dependency on an allegedly better candidate. Repeating the same negative cart_check returns the previous objection without producing a new way forward. A negative verdict should remain protective while a bounded dispute-resolution path evaluates its specific basis.

2. t-350bf2a022, chiffon top: the system rejects both sides of an alternative comparison.

The first 98-cent accepted item sells out. A later 297-cent cart is rejected because 75-cent alternatives allegedly qualify through clothing.material=chiffon. After the planner swaps to one of those alternatives, a subsequent review rejects it because material=net yarn takes precedence. The planner returns to the 297-cent cart, but its previous optimality rejection remains cached. The agent finishes neither by ordering nor by declining: three environment steps and $0.008712210 remain.

This is not evidence that the 297-cent item is definitely the answer. It is evidence that one review's claim that an alternative qualifies conflicts with a later review's claim that it does not, and that the cache has no explicit representation of this contradiction. cart_review_context includes the current cart's requirements, quantities and records; it excludes the records/availability/verdicts of the alternatives supporting an optimality rejection. Thus a claim about another candidate can outlive the evidence on which it depends.

A scoped solution is to distinguish rejection reasons and their dependencies. An optimality rejection should name the cheaper candidate and the public evidence that it qualifies. If that evidence changes or becomes disputed, mark the comparison unresolved and allow one compact adjudication. Do not automatically approve the expensive cart or discard every negative review simply because the planner objects.

3. t-abf1c45838, square clock: replacement is added, but the unavailable old line remains.

The accepted 53-cent clock sells out. The planner adds the earlier 62-cent candidate without removing the unavailable 53-cent line. Reconstructed final cart state contains both. terminal_recovery_hold correctly refuses to order the unavailable line, but finish repeatedly returns that instruction to the same unaffordable full-history planner. Seven steps and $0.008123160 remain. There is no transaction-shaped recovery that performs removal, verifies the repaired basket and finishes.

The fix should retain the stockout guard and execute a small, schema-validated recovery sequence through the world's existing cart tools. It should preserve requested quantities, remove the actually unavailable line, validate the replacement, and require real successful observations before treating repair as done. All calls must remain subject to the same monetary, step and message limits. No fixed tool names or product IDs should enter production logic.

4. t-b9e9292a88, facial cleanser: the last planner response is truncated before valid cart arguments are complete.

After an accepted cleanser sells out, the replacement planner names another candidate. Its admitted reply cap is only 609 tokens. It stops with finish_reason=length and an incomplete cart tool-call argument string. The planning loop normalizes the failed parse to an empty object, and schema validation prevents execution. Final state still contains the unavailable original line. Five steps and $0.008817095 remain. The unavailable-line guard is working; the recovery-output and follow-up path are not.

The safe response is a compact, bounded retry with enough visible-output capacity, rather than executing malformed arguments or sending another large conversation with a shrinking output allowance. The existing argument validation must remain intact. This case illustrates why admitting a request financially does not establish that the resulting token cap is sufficient for a usable action.

5. t-eb5b2c8367, monitor: the required-procedure decision has no visible output.

The cart receives a positive product review. One publicly sourced duty remains: tell the shopper what is worth checking before committing savings. procedure_gate requests max(700, 200+180*pending_duties), which is 700 for this one-duty state. Six calls each consume all 700 completion tokens as reasoning, return finish_reason=length, and contain no usable decision. No required message is executed. A local fix should provide adequate answer capacity for this procedure role, explicitly control reasoning where supported, and bound retries by relevant duty/cart/evidence state rather than allowing unrelated step changes to restart identical failures. It must still require an executed, successful procedure and actual evidence. Bypassing the gate, silently deleting the duty or trusting a proposed message as completed would sacrifice the original procedure protections.

OpenRouter's current reasoning documentation explains that reasoning consumes the same output budget and can leave empty content while still being billed. The most rational update sequence is:

A. Repair the full agent's procedure-output contract first. It affects only the one recorded pending-duty case; twenty-nine tasks have no duties. Use a sufficiently sized, budget-admitted decision, bounded retries, correctly identified local blocking status and actual action/proof verification. Test the six recorded truncations, malformed/unsupported responses, real-client compatibility, real 402 handling and existing procedure-proof tests. Preserve empty-duty behavior exactly. This issue is specific to the full ProcedureControls implementation; the budget-only variant retains the earlier procedure strategy.

B1. Add one budget-aware compact recovery handoff. Trigger it only when a genuine pending repair/no-progress state cannot advance through the ordinary planner. Preserve all active requirements, quantities, unavailable lines, relevant alternatives, duties and remaining resources. Execute only schema-valid supplied tools. Give it a small action/model-attempt bound and account each physical call through the existing ledger. Stop repeating an identical local hold through the same oversized conversation. This is the main shared improvement area.

B2. Resolve review dependencies and disagreements within that handoff. Keep negative reviews protective. Represent property failure separately from an optimality comparison, retain the evidence/candidate dependencies, invalidate only conclusions whose support changed, and allow one compact conflict resolution when appropriate. Never turn a planner assertion into checkout permission. Test the two contradictory-review histories and unchanged-context cache reuse separately from stockout repair.

B3. Close fallback gaps only after recovery is reachable. Distinguish a known empty cart from unknown cart state, require a current-cart review after material replacement, and do not treat a monetary refusal as proof that no item fits. Verify the clock's two-line cart, the cleanser's truncated arguments, the empty-cart BARE response and the unchecked long-sleeve replacement. This ordering prevents another update consisting solely of stricter guards and more unfinished tasks.

C. Add source-to-ledger requirement coverage as its own change. Validate the logo/exclusion omission against the actual request, plus multi-item constraints, alternatives, negations and withdrawals in the passing corpus. If a separate coverage call is needed, budget it explicitly; extending extraction metadata must not silently invalidate all old valid brief responses. Do not combine this prompt change with model routing or question-policy changes.

D. Study interview redundancy and wasted question-planning output separately. Keep all prior successful preference disclosures as controls and retain the existing planner while testing a narrowly changed role. The one needs-not-found trace does not justify a general ranking table or a wholesale interviewing rewrite.

Every step needs the current twenty-one passing tasks as mandatory preservation controls, plus the three observed v2-to-v3 regressions and the exact relevant failure snapshots. Test one component at a time and keep a source/hash checkpoint for each. Healthy paths should stay unchanged for the recovery-only stages. A recorded response cannot validate a new model decision; replay must stop and label that branch unobserved, rather than borrowing another run's answer. Any scored comparison must record paired wins/losses, completion, meaningful outputs, cost per correct task, physical attempts and latency tails, with world/shopper variation acknowledged.
