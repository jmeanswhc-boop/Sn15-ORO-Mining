TOSKY V2: IMPLEMENTATION AND OFFLINE REVIEW
Prepared 2026-11-13. Candidate: agents/Tosky_v2.py.

RESULT AND LIMITS

Created a separate single-file version from the full ICDI_chidori_v4 baseline submitted as Tosky. Eight previous ICDI_chidori versions, including both v4 variants, retain their recorded SHA-256 hashes. No paid inference, local validator evaluation, race entry or submission was performed. All 949 files in the prior three-agent study's manifest also retain their hashes.

The combined offline suite passes 269 tests: 147 tests of the unchanged v4 baseline, 76 new candidate tests and 46 reusable financial/transport/procedure checks retargeted to the candidate (including legacy source controls). The 147 baseline tests are not represented as tests of the new controller. Three complete simulated agent_main flows finish through the real ProxyClient with HTTP intercepted: ordinary order; a rejected checkout carrying a budget change followed by re-review and a successful order; and an evidence-supported decline. These are control-flow checks with scripted model replies, not 269 shopping successes or a new accuracy measurement.

The central change is the treatment of evidence and decision state. A proposed interpretation, an opened record, a completed reference path, a successful action and a usable final review are now represented separately. Counters limit work; exhausting a counter does not establish that a requirement has been resolved or that no suitable product exists. New terminal checks are accompanied by bounded next-hop, compact-continuation and repair paths.

This addresses the demonstrated implementation mechanisms from the public comparison. It cannot certify that every hidden cause of every zero has been eliminated: released race feedback omits the original private request and private checks, while a model can still misinterpret a valid quotation. The new agent has not earned a measured higher score yet. Neither AST parsing nor these tests certify the backend's structural-similarity decision. Those limits must remain explicit rather than being replaced by a promised perfect next race.

EVIDENCE COLLECTED AND HOW IT WAS USED

The preceding study contains all 540 released race feedback records and 180 qualifying feedback records for original CHIDORI, Tosky/v4 and Pooh. Their race totals were 35/180, 38/180 and 40/180; all six executions failed on 61 of 90 distinct race tasks. Tosky's 20 unfinished sessions all ended agent_stopped. Public records did not show a provider-cap termination, and its complete-run reported bills were $2.061962 and $1.998394. The inherited reference resolver and its handoff to finalization were shared weaknesses, rather than evidence that copying Pooh wholesale would remove the common failures.

For this implementation, six deliberately selected nonchampion sources and twelve included qualifying run indexes were downloaded, followed by all 72 target-task feedback records: six failed-at-least-once Tosky tasks, two executions and six comparison agents. Every qualifying index matches those task identities, catalog epoch w-2385fe7c5e6d, runtime 4.0.0 and contract oro.world.contract.8. This is a targeted public sample, not an exhaustive census of all entrants.

THE SIX QUALIFIER FAILURE CLASSES

1. Glasses: preserve capability coverage and permit a justified decline.

The successful comparison behavior is a decline. A narrow 0.1-0.4 capability does not establish coverage reaching +/-6. Treating a capability requirement as an upper permitted measurement can turn an unsuitable cheap product into an apparent match. The source audit and typed replies retain a separate coverage relation. Numeric checks reject provably narrower capabilities when units are comparable; they do not invent unit conversions. Missing evidence stays unknown. The decline audit accepts a decisive quoted mismatch while rejecting an unsupported claim that a known matching numeric candidate is unsuitable.

2. Single-pack cola: preserve property identity.

One cart unit and pack_size=1 do not establish the separately requested pack type. The passing control has explicit pack_type=single and packaging_type=cans. Final evidence must cite the actual requested property, with the correct component scope; a neighboring count field or a shared value cannot substitute for it. The product from that trace is not embedded in the agent. The same rule prevents frame-color evidence from proving a lens-color requirement.

3. Sugar-free cola: spend the interview on short, distinct questions.

A public passing reply answers a pack-size question by explicitly naming pack type. Interpretation must follow the reply's named property rather than force it into the asked label. New replies carry separate property, literal value, operator and source quotation. Runtime coverage/diversity ranking supplies an interview queue; initial questions stay short, and crowding feedback constrains width. Required procedure messages remain reserved. No fixed cola topics or category-specific priority list is installed.

4. Dining: discover the missing preference without memorizing it.

PRIMO's first two short questions receive indifference; its third asks about seats or table base and discovers a trestle requirement. Four Leaf also solves both attempts, while four of the other sampled agents fail both. This shows that the preference is discoverable, not that a dining-specific table-base rule should be hardcoded. The queue ranks actual observed runtime fields and offers backup topics; exclusions, alternatives and bounds do not automatically mark an entire property settled. The actual question selected by a future model remains unmeasured offline.

5. Sweater: separate the literal value from framing.

The public passing reply says it only accepts boat neck and adds conversational framing. Tosky's failed sibling interpretation searched framing phrases and declined after seeing a valid product. The original additive-only coverage audit could preserve an invented constraint. The new audit atomically covers every existing constraint and can correct or remove a falsely binding narrative clause with a source quotation. Reply values are separate quoted spans, not a whole unfiltered sentence. An impossible but actual requirement is never removed merely because no current record satisfies it.

6. Footwear: keep narrative from becoming a product requirement.

The earlier comparison exposed interpretation of figurative stage language as a performance-use constraint. The source audit distinguishes context from an actual product property while preserving real exclusions, alternatives and scope. All six comparison agents solve both sampled executions, so this is not a reason to introduce a new special footwear architecture. The generic intake/reply correction handles the mechanism.

RACE AND OPERATING FAILURE MECHANISMS

A. Incomplete reference paths and wrong same-seller pages.

The new reference tracker stores each item/need independently: the full locator, selector contract, relationship, accepted opened page, exact citations, onward edge and verified terminal value. The selector description is obtained before choosing among candidates. Every described selector must then be covered; another product from the same seller is insufficient. A terminal result requires the requested property on an opened page, and cannot ignore a detected onward instruction. Placeholders, empty last-find fields and exhausted hold counters do not resolve a reference. Cycles and repeated action probes are bounded. Changed evidence invalidates the dependent path. Relationships such as different, bounds, within and explicit pairing are retained rather than flattened to equality; semantic interpretation of those relationships still requires the model.

The saved successful Tosky jacket chain remains a preservation fixture, including its public onward directions. Tests check the acceptance of a fully opened two-hop path, rejection of wrong/unread/changed pages and counter exhaustion, and preservation of a negative relationship. They do not replay a future model or claim a newly recovered race point.

B. False decline after reading a viable candidate.

The public watch case shows Tosky declining after repeated empty keyword searches despite a previously read fitting numeric candidate. A new decline audit revisits opened candidates and actual requirements, with runtime search receipts and quoted mismatch evidence. Empty keyword results, local inference refusal or a counter limit cannot independently prove infeasibility. The old positive-candidate audit is retained. A justified decline can still finish, as verified by the complete no-purchase flow.

The shortlist and search-coverage assessment are bounded and model-dependent; they are not a mathematical proof of global store infeasibility. The code does not claim otherwise. New known records invalidate a cached decline judgment.

C. Local budget refusal and repeated holds without progress.

When the ordinary tool-planning history cannot be admitted, a compact state packet can continue through the same financial ledger. It includes the request, live changes, unresolved references, current procedures, observed records, cart and latest review faults. It has a bounded number of states and requests. Repeated holds hand off to repair rather than repeatedly sending the same large history. The initial brief interpretation also has a finite attempt bound. None of these paths can reopen a remote 402 or pretend a local hold was an executed action.

D. Unsupported cart identities and false procedure receipts.

Nested cart schemas are checked against observed record identities before the environment consumes a step. Enum verbs and ordinary free-text notes are not misclassified as IDs. An explicit failed action is removed from procedure/selection evidence. Binding propagation, consecutive step numbers, one action per request, replay behavior and the finished check remain on the existing transport path. The complete-flow tests also verify that inference authorization does not leak into environment calls.

E. Unknown, stale or contradictory final reviews.

Every order, including forced/fallback completion, needs a usable current evidence review. A bare ok flag, missing output, empty/null quoted field or an incomplete assignment does not pass. The review checks the actual opened item, quantity, finite known price, availability, budget, every live requirement and relevant known alternatives. A bounded fresh adjudication can repair an unavailable or mistaken review; negative state is not an unconditional permanent ban. The preparatory finish check is distinguished from an actual decline so that a valid fallback order remains possible.

F. Optimality and stale alternatives.

Review state includes known alternative records for non-any objectives. Discovering a cheaper candidate invalidates a positive cached review. For a single requested item, an opened available cheaper same-category candidate that provably meets every exact-property requirement cannot be ignored even if the model returns objective.ok=true. The broader ranking interpretation remains model-dependent. This fixes a cache bug uncovered during integration; it is not a claim of complete optimal search.

G. Changes, omitted duties and procedure approvals.

Source audits cover explicit workflow duties as well as product requirements. Real replies from procedure messages are retained and interpreted before a terminal action. Within one replacement, withdrawal is applied before the new value is added. Source-step tracking prevents stale replies from reversing a newer binding; explicit later reaffirmation can restore a withdrawn value. Unquoted additions and uninterpreted notices remain pending. A newly changed task must satisfy its current procedure gate before ending. Existing cart-dependent approval and message reservations remain intact.

H. Price recognition on nonpaginated searches.

The first complete-flow test exposed an inherited dependency: price/stock field recognition could occur only through pagination-shape learning. A nonpaginated result could therefore leave a correct cart without a recognized price. A small schema adapter now learns exact financial keys from observed records independently of pagination, with bounded attempts. It does not relax the requirement for a finite nonnegative known price.

FINANCIAL REVIEW

The unchanged local allowance is $0.035 per task, or $3.15 for 90 tasks, with a $0.010 protected procedure reserve within each task's allocation. This leaves a nominal $1.85 below a $5 run cap for shared costs and estimation error; it is not an additional agent allowance. The cap is per validator run, not a combined two-validator account budget. ORO's current public documentation confirms $2 qualifying and $5 race scoped-key caps.

All three existing model IDs are in the saved current ORO allowlist. Their base and long-context pricing tiers match the saved primary OpenRouter catalog after normalizing pricing.overrides. No model substitution or allowance increase was made. New structured roles use at most two physical transmissions per decision and disable unnecessary reasoning, with one compatibility fallback still inside the attempt accounting. Unknown charges retain their reservations; a remote 402 stops inference.

Packet checks show the new compact continuation is admissible after $0.020 has been spent in the tested state, reserving $0.001265 for a 1,600-token output cap. The structured proof packets in the complete-flow samples also remain admissible at that balance. An inherited heavy decline-audit packet can be refused; a new compact review must not be mistaken for permission to bypass the ledger. Financial packet details are saved in financial-verification.json.

The quote uses a conservative byte envelope and configured prices. It is not a certified tokenizer, routed-price upper bound or full-run billing receipt. The actual next 90-task cost and latency cannot be measured by assigning fake low costs in a test; the report makes no such forecast. The full cap, score, runtime tails and gains/losses against the old agent remain unmeasured until a separately authorized evaluation.

SUBMISSION AND PRESERVATION REVIEW

The candidate is UTF-8, 630,713 bytes, with one synchronous agent_main. Its 56-method controller is on Episode's live inheritance path; the existing 362 class methods retain their AST bodies. The original files remain byte-identical. Local ast.parse completed in approximately 0.17 seconds. The static checks find only the existing permitted imports, no new file operations, dynamic execution, direct networking, concrete benchmark identifiers or newly added fixed product/attribute/unit tables.

This is meaningful reachable functionality rather than cosmetic changes or unused padding. The private server's anti-plagiarism/integrity analyzer was not invoked, and local checks cannot guarantee its admission decision. Public code requirements were refreshed during the review. No credentials, hidden evaluation data or operator procedures were added to public tracked code.
