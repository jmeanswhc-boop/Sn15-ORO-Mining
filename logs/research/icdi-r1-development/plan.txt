ICDI_r1: KYO v6 baseline, original preserved. Offline development only.

Objective: reliable state transitions under ambiguous requests, misunderstood
questions and revised requirements in unfamiliar ORO worlds. No public score
target, task identifiers, catalog mappings or private grader logic in the agent.

1. Reproduce baseline defects with transport-disabled tests. Inspect released
   race observations and successor implementations; separate trace evidence
   from hypotheses and synthetic counterexamples.
2. Normalize explicitly structured text observations without dropping raw data.
   Apply customer replies and store notes through one validated delta reducer.
   Track pending interpretations and invalidate dependent decisions on changes.
3. Ask sequentially, repair confusion once, distinguish unknown from indifference.
   Carry unresolved ambiguity into the planner. Enforce evidenced procedures and
   review verdicts at every terminal path, including recovery.
4. Reserve inference cost before every physical request, account for failures and
   missing usage, and protect a bounded recovery allowance. Keep existing models.
5. Combine control-flow simulations, before/after race-observation replays,
   no-change controls and budget stress checks. Check syntax, permitted imports,
   entry point, proxy-only I/O, file size and original hashes. Publish limitations.

Advance stages only after their checks pass. Offline mock outcomes establish
controller behavior, not live semantic accuracy or future hidden-race scores.
The user will arrange paid testing; no submission or inference is authorized here.
