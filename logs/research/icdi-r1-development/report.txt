ICDI_r1 — implementation and offline review

Outcome
-------
Created agents/ICDI_r1.py from the archived public KYO v6 source with the user's
explicit baseline selection. The original is unchanged. No paid model call,
evaluation, submission, dashboard restart or repository commit was performed.

48 offline tests pass. Four complete scripted interaction flows pass. This is
an offline-verified candidate for the user's paid validation, not evidence of
a higher race score or a promise of championship performance.

Scope and evidence
------------------
The target is reliability as the observed shopping state evolves: interpreting
unfamiliar observation formats, clarifying incomplete or misunderstood customer
instructions, applying explicit changes, and keeping purchase decisions aligned
with the current requirements. Public leaderboard scores were not an optimization
target. No suite answers, product vocabulary tables or grader logic were added.

The two locally preserved KYO race-24 run records have 90 completed episodes each,
with 18 and 19 correct respectively. These are historical baseline observations,
not an identification of the user's reported 5% result. Only two detailed KYO
feedback files were present locally for this race. Both report
process_requirement_missed, and neither includes the initial query. They cannot
establish the prevalence of ambiguity or changes across the complete race.

Nevertheless, the released observations reveal a concrete portability problem:
listing records and their attributes are often embedded in text. Replaying every
observation through KYO's original ingestion produces zero attribute records in
both saved traces. Replaying them through the candidate produces 14 and 18
attribute records respectively (40 and 154 listing records). These are ingestion
counts, not newly solved tasks. They establish that evidence previously absent
from the ledger is now available for questioning and review.

One archived interaction also contains three confused replies followed by questions
on different properties. The new interview protocol explicitly distinguishes a
confused answer from indifference and can repair a question before moving on.
Whether the model correctly makes this distinction on unseen wording remains
for paid verification.

The race filenames and reproduction results are recorded in
02-observation-replay.json. The excerpts are used only as observations, not as
embedded answers or instructions in the submission.

Critical review of successors
-----------------------------
Inspected PIRATE and Pooh's relevant ingestion, reply-update and procedure paths,
as well as PIRATE's questioning prompt and reply correction path. PIRATE gives
more attention to observed attribute coverage, differing values and contextual
hints; it also tries to repair specific semantic interpretation mistakes. These
are useful ideas, but better historical results do not validate every inherited
method. In the inspected source, the dict/list-oriented ingestion, divergent
reply/note reducers, and permissive procedure completion patterns remain.

The candidate therefore retains evidence-informed question planning and the
baseline's search, comparison and candidate machinery, and supplies the missing
text observations to it. It does not transplant a successor's large collection
of semantic heuristics. A narrow fix to the shared observation/update boundary
is more directly testable for this iteration. The earlier local Tosky v6 budget
and review work was also consulted as a design reference, not used as a new
baseline. TOSKY.py was not edited or resumed.

Stages and results
------------------
1. Baseline reproduction.
   Created 15 transport-disabled behavioral tests; 13 failed on unmodified KYO
   and two preservation controls passed. The failures cover budget-only updates,
   replacement/reinstatement, invalid item scope, missed or consumed notes,
   quantities, review invalidation, text ingestion, and permissive reviews.
   Evidence: 01-baseline-tests.txt.

2. Observation and state updates.
   Added one general syntax adapter for JSON strings and explicit pipe-separated
   fields, including nested key=value records. Names and values are learned from
   the observation; there is no fixed product or attribute registry. The raw pipe
   record is retained alongside its parsed view. Duplicate field names remain raw
   rather than being silently overwritten. Native object observations remain
   semantically unchanged. The adapter is used for ordinary and internal calls.

   Interview replies, store notes and approval replies use one delta format and
   one reducer. Supported changes include firm needs, explicit replacements and
   removals, reinstatement, quantities, budget, budget scope/unit, objective and
   stock/decline policy. Item indices are validated instead of clamped. Compatible
   additional bounds are retained. Reference requirements are updated with the
   active constraints; a retired reference loses its resolved value.

   Changed decisions are invalidated: review results, approval caches, stale
   automatic replacement candidates and confirmed-cart shortcuts. Observed
   listings and unrelated requirements are preserved. Budget-only changes now
   invalidate a review. A review key also includes current quantities, ledger,
   evidence and reference values, so changed price/stock is not hidden behind an
   unchanged product ID.

   Every production semantic update requires source quotes; fabricated quotes
   or invalid shapes are rejected. Quotes establish provenance, not semantic
   correctness. Failed interpretations remain pending and can be retried; they
   are not silently marked consumed. A claimed change with no represented effect
   cannot be acknowledged as applied.

   All 12 stage-specific state/observation tests passed before moving forward.
   Evidence: 02-state-tests.txt, 02-observation-replay.json.

3. Clarification and terminal review.
   The initial interpretation can retain consequential ambiguous instructions as
   sourced questions without inventing a firm value. Ordinary undisclosed
   preferences are not automatically treated as blocking ambiguity.

   Questions are sent and interpreted sequentially. Later questions skip properties
   already answered or explicitly left open. Confusion can receive one simpler
   retry per property in that interview, within the message/step allowance. Failed
   model parsing retries the interpretation, not the shopper message. Explicit
   unresolved instructions retain priority over speculative preference discovery.

   Every message required on the longer terminal procedure path is reserved,
   including pre-choice duties. The old maximum-of-single-messages rule could
   reserve one message even when two separate required steps each needed one.

   Procedures require coverage of every due step and actual current record lines.
   Missing, partial, malformed or stale proof does not grant permission. Approval
   replies enter the same update reducer; a changed requirement returns control
   for replanning. Ordinary purchase and recovery use the same gates. Negative
   reviews remain negative on repeated attempts; malformed reviews are unknown,
   not approvals. Explicit holds are returned to the planner as such instead of
   being described as network failures.

   The first 20 cumulative checks passed after this stage. Evidence: 03-before.txt
   and 03-after.txt. Later combined checks cover additional preservation risks.

4. Inference budget admission.
   The original spend thresholds were checked after calls, could be exceeded by
   recovery, and did not reliably account for missing usage or every retry.

   Every physical inference attempt now obtains a reservation before transmission.
   Text bytes plus framing overhead conservatively estimate prompt tokens. Model
   rate envelopes include cache-write/long-prompt allowances and a 25% margin.
   Output is bounded before the request; requests without useful output room are
   refused and can fall back to an affordable model. Existing model IDs remain.

   Actual finite nonnegative reported cost settles the reservation. Missing usage
   or uncertain transport keeps the full reservation charged. Every physical
   attempt counts. Credit exhaustion stops retries immediately. A charge or token
   count exceeding its envelope stops further inference instead of continuing
   with a knowingly invalid estimate. Transient retries are bounded to one per
   model attempt.

   Allocation: $0.050 per invocation, of which $0.006 is protected until recovery.
   This gives $4.50 for exactly 90 invocations and $1.50 for exactly 30. No shared
   file, global cross-task balance, phase detection or validator-specific routing
   is used. The same strategy applies to every task.

   The missing-usage stress test, including the recovery allowance, permits 15
   physical requests in its fixture and accounts for $0.04796775 per task:
   $4.3170975 for 90 identical invocations. This is a reservation test, not a
   forecast of actual run cost. Unexpected provider prices, billing behavior or
   additional task invocations can invalidate the aggregate bound. The provider's
   scoped key remains the authoritative cap.

   Six budget regressions failed before the change; all passed afterward.
   Evidence: 04-before.txt, 04-after.txt, static-review.json.

5. Combined review and verification.
   Added cross-stage controls for multi-item preservation, independent numeric
   bounds, obsolete references, idempotent replay, raw observation retention,
   product-description separation, source validation, current approval evidence,
   partial procedures and cached review outcomes.

   Combined testing exposed queued actions continuing after the first action's
   observation changed the requirements. The remaining actions now receive tool
   results explaining that replanning is required; no environment step is spent
   executing the obsolete plan. A delimiter case involving a URL inside a value
   and malformed update/procedure identifiers were also corrected before final
   verification.

   Full agent_main flows use only scripted proxy clients and fictional products:
     ordinary purchase:              6 environment steps, 6 scripted model calls
     text-form listing records:      6 environment steps, 6 scripted model calls
     checkout requirement change:    12 environment steps, 12 scripted model calls
     required approval:              7 environment steps, 9 scripted model calls
   The ordinary unchanged baseline control also takes 6 steps and 6 model calls.
   These counts show fixture behavior, not real-model latency or cost.

   All 48 final tests pass. See verification.txt and static-review.json.
   Reproduce with: python3 -B -m unittest agents.tests.test_icdi_r1 -v
   The test harness removes only the ProxyClient import and supplies clients that
   cannot contact a service. No credentials are read or needed.

Submission review
-----------------
The candidate parses as Python, is UTF-8, has one synchronous agent_main, and is
181,869 bytes / 2,923 lines. It retains precisely the baseline import set: json,
random, re, time, typing.Any, future annotations and the supported ProxyClient.
No filesystem operations, direct network calls, encoding modules, eval/exec,
wall-clock strategy routing, product/unit tables or grader imports were added.
Time is used only for retry sleep. Decisions depend on the task and observations.
All four new controller methods and the observation adapter are reachable.
67 original controller methods retain identical ASTs; 24 are changed.

This is local review against the public rules, not execution of ORO's private
server-side integrity/similarity checker. Meaningful logic was added, but server
acceptance cannot be guaranteed without that service's response.

Boundaries and paid-verification handoff
--------------------------------------
The next paid result must determine semantic accuracy, not merely legal endings.
Inspect failures at the first incorrect observation/interpretation/update/action
transition. Separate misunderstood requirements, undiscovered preferences,
changes missed, procedure mistakes, wrong optimization, noncompletion and
resource exhaustion. These categories can overlap; one reported failure category
is not a complete causal inventory.

Use identical practice cases and seed for a baseline/candidate comparison where
possible, with repeated comparisons before attributing a small difference to
code. Practice/qualifying results remain regression signals, not estimates of a
new hidden world. Actual subsequent race feedback is the competitive evidence.

Remaining limitations:
- Semantic extraction, property prioritization, equivalence and procedure evidence
  interpretation still depend on model accuracy. Source quotes are not proof of
  correct understanding; the offline replies do not test live model behavior.
- The text adapter handles explicit supported syntax, not all possible prose or
  markup. Unrecognized text remains visible but may not populate the ledger.
- Added/removed product categories and arbitrary new workflow instructions are not
  represented by a general item/workflow editing engine in this scoped iteration.
- Few allowed messages may make all hidden needs impossible to discover. A bounded
  clarification retry also consumes an opportunity to ask another property.
- The stricter purchase checks can increase unfinished tasks if interpretation or
  review cannot be recovered within the remaining resources. Four passing fixture
  flows and preservation controls do not establish absence of unseen regressions.
- Requirement revisions invalidate decision caches conservatively. Unrelated raw
  product evidence is retained, but some reviews/procedure checks may repeat.
- No measured improvement in race accuracy, inference cost or runtime is claimed.
